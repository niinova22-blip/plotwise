**Promotional text** (170 max, can change without review):

> Meet Ozan, a warm lantern-carrying mentor who talks you through a problem in five minutes and leaves you with one small experiment to try.

**Description:**

```
Stuck on a decision? Plotwise turns the problem in your head into one small, concrete next step, in about five minutes.

Your guide is Ozan, a warm old storyteller with a lantern. He talks you through each step out loud, reacts to what you write, and never lectures.

THREE WAYS TO THINK
• Thought Lab: describe a problem, uncover the quiet assumptions behind it, turn it around, and leave with a low-risk experiment for the next 48 hours.
• Future Simulator: explore three possible futures for a goal, make decisions along the way, and see what changes when something unexpected happens. Exploration, not prediction.
• Daily Training: short exercises in seeing alternatives, inverting a problem, connecting unrelated ideas and deciding with missing information.

A NOTEBOOK THAT REMEMBERS
Every session ends with a card: a questioned assumption, a new angle or an experiment. Three days later Ozan asks how your experiment went. When a new problem touches an old one, he reminds you what you learned.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up.
• Everything stays on your iPhone. Ozan's understanding runs on the device.
• No ads, no tracking.

Quiet mode hides Ozan for a compact text view. English and Turkish.

Plotwise is a thinking-practice tool, not therapy or professional advice. If you are in crisis, please contact local emergency services.
```
